Cars for Homes

Donated used cars, given through the Foundation directly to people who need one to live in and to get to work. Free and clear, titled in their name. No loan to lose.

Why this matters

When people first lose their home, many hold on to the one thing they have left: the car. It is a bed, a locker and the way to work. So they keep paying the car loan. Then one payment is missed, the car is repossessed, and they are on the street with nothing. The spiral is fast.

A car that is owned outright cannot be taken for a missed payment. That is the whole idea.

How it works

  1. You donate a used car to the Foundation for Job Creation, a 501(c)(3). It should run and have a clear title.
  2. We match it to an approved applicant in the same area.
  3. The car is given directly to that person, titled in their name, free and clear. It is not sold at auction.

Who can receive a car

  • A valid driver's license.
  • No DUI on their driving record.
  • Able to afford insurance, and to show proof of it before the car is handed over.
  • Homeless or about to be, and needing a car to get to work or to look for it.

Applicants agree to a check of their driving record before a car is handed over.

For donors: the tax difference

When a charity sells a donated car, the donor can usually deduct only what it sold for. When the charity gives the car directly to a person in need, as this program does, the donor may be able to deduct the car's fair market value. The Foundation reports this to you on IRS Form 1098-C. See IRS Publication 4303, and check with your tax adviser.

Where the program stands: we are now collecting car pledges and applications. No car is promised until one is matched and the checks are done. Cars are matched as they come in.
