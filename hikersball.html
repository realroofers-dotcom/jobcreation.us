The Hikers Ball

Formal dances held across the country for hikers and the people who staff homeless shelters — the hardest job in this fight, and the one nobody throws a party for.

Scan for thehikersball.com
Dates, venues, and how to attend or sponsor a night. Site in development.

The fifth Hikers Ball: Saturday, November 13, 2027. Location to be announced. The Ball has been held four times, always for the sake of the homeless. This year it is also a holiday kickoff art show and sale. Artists who want to show and sell their work, get in touch.
We are looking for a band and a hall. If you can donate either, or help pay for one, please call 732-995-3914 or email us. Donations are deeply appreciated.

Upcoming Balls

All guests are requested to bring new, clean women’s undergarments as a donation, for distribution to shelters. Some people refer to The Hikers Ball as the Underwear Ball.

Who it’s for

Shelter workers do the work almost nobody else will. They handle the overnight shifts, the overdoses, the intake at 2 a.m., the person who comes back for the fourth time. They are underpaid, they burn out, and they are thanked almost never. We welcome all shelter workers, but we need firm RSVPs — so watch for your area.

The Hikers Ball is a formal night held in their honor. Like a prom. Not a fundraiser dressed up as a party — a party, for them, with the room on their side.

However, donations and company sponsorships would be greatly appreciated. The homeless crisis hits all ages, and for many different reasons. If you are not homeless and never have been, count yourself as fortunate — we could always use your monetary help.

Admission is a ticket fee, and the fee covers food, drink, and music. These are very formal events. Separately, every guest is asked to bring new, clean women’s undergarments, which go directly to shelters — the single most requested item at intake and one of the least donated. Everyone remembers that price of admission, which is exactly the point.

Across the country

Four Balls so far. The Ball is meant to travel, held in different cities and honoring the shelter staff in each one. The fifth is set for November 13, 2027; the location will be announced here.

Part of the Foundation

The Hikers Ball is a program of the Foundation for Job Creation. TheHikersBall.com is in development and will carry dates, venues, and how to attend or sponsor a night.

If you run a shelter and want the Ball brought to your city, or you want to sponsor one, call.
