What we do

Research

Homelessness

On one night in January 2025, 745,652 Americans had no home. One word covers a veteran in a tent and a working mother in a car — and that single word is why so many responses miss. We are beginning the work of telling them apart.

Training

RooferSchool

Mobile trade training that travels to the cities where the need is greatest. Roofing first — then the part nobody teaches: how to price a job, run the business, and invest what you earn.
