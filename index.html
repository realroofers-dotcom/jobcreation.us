How it started

One question, asked in 2009

The Foundation was started by Mark Nejmeh — a roofer since 1978 who had run his own commercial roofing company — in the wreckage of the financial crisis, and self-funded from the first day.

Everyone was arguing about who caused the crash. Almost nobody was asking the question that mattered to people losing work: which companies actually create American jobs, and which ones only say they do?

The crash took the workforce that builds houses, not just the houses. Crews broke up, apprenticeships stopped, and what followed in a lot of those towns was an opioid story rather than a jobs story. Everything the Foundation does now — the company research, the trade training, the homelessness work, the community — comes out of that.
