Who is behind this

Mark Nejmeh, founder

A roofer since 1978 who built his own commercial roofing company, started this Foundation in the wreckage of the 2008 crash, and has paid for it himself ever since. Hiker, inventor, writer, lacrosse goalie, three-time heart attack survivor. He asks the questions nobody else is asking, and then he goes and does something about them.
