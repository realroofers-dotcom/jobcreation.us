Cars for Homeless · Application

Apply for a car

For people who are homeless or about to be, and who need a car to live in and to get to work. Read the requirements, then answer every question honestly.

Before you apply

  • You must be 18 or older and hold a valid driver’s license.
  • You must have no DUI on your driving record.
  • You must agree to a background check and a drug test before a car is given, and sign the authorization forms when we send them.
  • You must be able to insure the car, show proof of insurance before it is handed over, and keep it insured with a valid license for as long as you drive it.
  • Cars come from donors, and donors choose who receives their car. Approval does not guarantee a car.
