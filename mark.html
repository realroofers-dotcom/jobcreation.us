About the founder

Mark Nejmeh

Roofer, founder, hiker, writer. The Foundation for Job Creation is his, paid for out of his own pocket since 2009, and this site is as much about him as about the work.

What drives him

Mark has been on roofs since 1978. He built Continental Roof Systems, a commercial roofing company, and later Realroofers. When the 2008 crash broke up the crews that build America's houses, he started the Foundation for Job Creation to ask one plain question: which companies actually create American jobs, and which only say they do?

That question led straight to the stock market, where he has spent years reading the filings of small public companies and calling out the financings that quietly wipe out ordinary shareholders. It led to teaching the trade he knows, to studying homelessness up close, and to getting people outdoors and talking.
